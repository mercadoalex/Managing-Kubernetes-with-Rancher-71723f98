With Rancher installed, this lesson takes you on a guided tour of the Rancher dashboard. You will learn to navigate the cluster explorer, understand the global and cluster-level views, and configure basic settings like authentication and default roles.

<!-- [image] rancher-dashboard.png - Screenshot of the Rancher dashboard highlighting the main navigation areas -->

## Main Sections of the UI

The lesson covers the main sections of the UI:

- The **cluster dashboard** with resource summaries and health status
- **Workload** views for deployments, pods, and services
- **Storage** and **networking** configuration
- The **global settings** panel

<!-- [image] rancher-cluster-explorer.png - Screenshot of the cluster explorer view -->

By the end, you will be comfortable finding your way around and ready to start managing real workloads.
