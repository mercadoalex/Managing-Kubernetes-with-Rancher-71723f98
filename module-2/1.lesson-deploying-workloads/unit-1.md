This lesson teaches you how to deploy and manage applications through Rancher. You will create deployments, expose them via services, configure environment variables and secrets, and manage scaling - all through the Rancher UI and kubectl.

<!-- [image] rancher-workload-create.png - Screenshot of the workload creation form in Rancher -->

## From Deployment to Traffic

The lesson walks through deploying a sample application, setting up ingress rules, and verifying that traffic reaches the right pods. You will also learn how Rancher's workload management compares to raw kubectl operations.
